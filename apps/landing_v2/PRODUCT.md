# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitor to this marketing surface: restaurant owners and operators evaluating floor software, usually after a busy service, looking for visibility rather than another POS.

Other people the product serves, confirmed by the landing copy and strategy: walk-in guests (availability), seated guests (table request), waiters (live floor), owners (venue setup).

## Product Purpose

Seatd is restaurant floor operations software. It shows table status live, lets guests request help from a table QR without installing an app, and keeps staff looking at one picture of the room.

Success for this landing: a qualified owner requests a demo. Success for the product: the floor is easier to run during busy service.

## Positioning

Territory is the physical restaurant floor. Seatd is not a POS, reservation book, digital menu, ordering system, or loyalty app. The claim a neighbor cannot copy: one live visual floor plus guest assistance that does not require a guest app, sitting beside the systems the venue already uses.

## Operating Context

Busy hospitality floors with more than one service area (dining, patio, bar, lounge). Staff work from a live floor, including handheld. An entrance display can show where seating is available. Guests scan a table QR. Demo content on the landing uses a synthetic venue named Marlowe's.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed in the current landing and strategy:

- Live visual floor with available, occupied, and attention states
- Guest table assistance via QR, no guest app or account
- Multi-area awareness
- Public availability view at the entrance
- Complementary to POS (transactions) and reservations (the book)

Undecided or must not be fabricated: analytics or “Improve” claims if they have not shipped; pricing amounts; customer names, logos, or testimonials; fake-precise occupancy percentages.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Seatd. Existing lockups, symbol, photography, and the incumbent landing identity in `apps/landing_v2` are binding unless a later redesign is approved. Voice is operational and concrete, not SaaS hype. Demo venue Marlowe's is labeled synthetic by its role as demonstration data.

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand marks: `public/brand/`
- Venue photography: `public/images/seatd-*.webp`
- Interactive floor demonstration: `lib/demo-floor.ts` plus `LiveFloor`, `ZoneFloor`, `HeroServiceMoment`
- No customer logos, case studies, or named testimonials on hand. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- The live floor is the product proof. It must read as a restaurant you could walk, not a packed diagram.
- Translate features into operational outcomes. Do not sell “real-time table management.”
- Do not claim to replace POS or reservations.
- Do not present unshipped intelligence as current product.
- Guests never have to download an app to ask for help.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific legal standard was recorded. The marketing site must remain keyboardable, honor reduced motion, and keep text contrast at WCAG AA.
